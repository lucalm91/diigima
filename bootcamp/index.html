Meet Your Tutors

Our duo at Diigima Creative Studio combines years of writing, directing, filming and editing experience to guide you through each phases of the bootcamp. They also run storytelling and video-making courses for various cultural and corporate organizations.

Cal Smyth

With over 20 years experience as a script writer, crime fiction novelist, and video director, Cal merges creativity with practical teaching.

Luca Lamorte

With over 10 years behind the camera, in the recording studio, and in the editing suite, Luca combines his tech know-how with hands-on learning.
