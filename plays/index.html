Theatre & Performance

The Magic of the Stage, Preserved Forever.

From studio-created trailers to in-theatre set-ups, we film actors to capture their powerhouse performances. With our creative and technical experience, we bridge the gap between the live experience of theatre and the screen, creating promotional materials or archival recordings that do justice to your art.

Why Work With Us?

Low-Light Expertise

Stage lighting is challenging for cameras. We use specialized lenses and sensors to capture rich, noise-free images that respect your lighting design.

Crystal Clear Audio

A play lives and dies by its dialogue. We use multi-track recording to ensure every whisper and shout is captured with pristine clarity.

Multi-Angle Coverage

We shoot with multiple cameras to catch the wide ensemble moments and the intimate close-ups simultaneously, giving you the best of both worlds.

Promotional Edits

Need to sell tickets fast? We deliver short, punchy teaser clips within 24 hours of the shoot to boost your marketing while the show is running.
