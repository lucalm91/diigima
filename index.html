How we create videos

Pre-production

We listen to you. With years of interview experience, we find out what your story is and what you’d like to get across to your audience.

Whether it is a one-off trailer or a long-running series, we use our script writing skills to shape the narrative for your video content.

Filming

If we need to film on location, we find the right place. If it’s in-house, we come to you. And if it’s studio-based, we arrange that.

As the Diigima-duo, we cover every aspect of lights, camera, action to create the right atmosphere and make captivating videos.

Post-production

Combining our storytelling and tech skill-sets, we make sure the final edits match the original stories as we take on board your feedback.

With soundtracks, voice overs and captions in place, we deliver high-quality video content primed to captivate your audience.

About Diigima

From cave drawings to cinema movies to phone-screen videos, humans are captivated by visual stories. And at Diigima Creative Studio, we’re driven by creative collaborations.

With trailers, short films, videos, animations, audio stories, podcasts and interactive quests produced across digital platforms, Cal Smyth and Luca Lamorte are the Diigima duo.

We took the name Diigima from the Greek for narrative or short form story. It’s our passion, imagination, dedication and desire to get audio-video stories out into the world.

Cal Smyth

Cal is the creative guy – writing scripts, directing films and authoring novels. His storytelling started when he was five years old, writing his first book in red felt-tip called Cops and Robbers. Since then, he’s written multiple crime novels and scripts, with a Balkan Noir series published and a sci-fi romantic thriller coming out soon.

  •     LinkedIn    

Luca Lamorte

Luca is the tech-whiz – behind the camera, in the editing suite and designing websites. As a student, he gained his first editing job when he was hitch-hiking along Costa Brava. Since then, he’s used his technical skills to create everything from websites, landing pages and user journeys to recording podcasts, filming and editing videos.

Luma Freitas

Luma is the business strategist – turning creative ideas into successful partnerships. Her journey began in customer relations, where she mastered the art of listening. Since then, she’s been dedicated to connecting clients with the perfect storytelling solutions.
