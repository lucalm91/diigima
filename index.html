How we create videos

Pre-production

  • We listen to you. With years of interview experience, we find out what your story is and what you’d like to get across to your audience.
  • Whether it is a one-off trailer or a long-running series, we use our script writing skills to shape the narrative for your video content.

Filming

  • If we need to film on location, we find the right place. If it’s in-house, we come to you. And if it’s studio-based, we arrange that.
  • As the Diigima-duo, we cover every aspect of lights, camera, action to create the right atmosphere and make captivating videos.

Post-production

  • Combining our storytelling and tech skill-sets, we make sure the final edits match the original stories as we take on board your feedback.
  • With soundtracks, voice overs and captions in place, we deliver high-quality video content primed to captivate your audience.

About Diigima

We are a Barcelona-based, audio-video service that merges personalized storytelling & digital know-how to create captivating video content.

Cal Smyth

With five novels published & multiple scripts filmed as trailers, videos and audio stories across digital platforms, Cal is driven by creative collaboration. Having worked in education, media & marketing, he has over 20 years’ experience as a writer, interviewer & storyteller. Originally from the UK, Cal has lived in Serbia, Japan, Italy, Mexico & the Netherlands before settling in Barcelona.

Luca Lamorte

Whether it is filming interviews, events or trailers, Luca isn’t just behind the camera, but also at the computer and working his magic in the editing suite. With over 10 years’ experience in the entertainment, marketing & tech industries, he loves applying his digital and tech know-how to create video content. Originally from Italy, Luca has been living in Barcelona for several years.
